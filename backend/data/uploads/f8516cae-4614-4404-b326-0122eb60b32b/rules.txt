COMPANY RULES AND REGULATIONS

1. WORKING HOURS
   - Standard hours are 9:00 AM to 6:00 PM, Monday to Friday.
   - Flexible start times between 8:00 AM and 10:00 AM are allowed with manager approval.
   - Overtime must be pre-approved and logged in the system.

2. LEAVE POLICY
   - Annual leave: 20 days per year for full-time employees.
   - Sick leave: Up to 10 days per year with medical certificate if absent more than 2 consecutive days.
   - Leave requests must be submitted at least 2 weeks in advance.

3. CONDUCT AND ETHICS
   - All employees must maintain confidentiality of company and client information.
   - Use of company resources for personal gain is prohibited.
   - Discrimination, harassment, or bullying will result in disciplinary action.

4. REMOTE WORK
   - Remote work is permitted up to 2 days per week with team lead approval.
   - Employees must be available during core hours (10:00 AM–4:00 PM) and respond within 2 hours.

5. DATA SECURITY
   - Passwords must be changed every 90 days and must meet complexity requirements.
   - Sensitive data must not be stored on personal devices or sent via personal email.
   - Report any security incidents to IT within 24 hours.

Effective date: January 2025. Revisions require HR and management approval.
